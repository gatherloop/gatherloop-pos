# Order Checkout (QRIS & Cash)

## What it does

[Table ordering](/sales/table-ordering) got a customer from a scanned QR code to a full cart. This closes the loop: the cart page — already showing the guest their items and total — is also the payment page. Its footer button reads **"Bayar dengan QRIS · {total}"**; there's no separate checkout page recapping the same cart first. Tapping it asks the guest's name (prefilled if they've ordered from this phone before) and, when both payment methods are enabled, a choice between them: **"Bayar dengan QRIS"** or **"Bayar dengan Cash di Kasir"**. Choosing QRIS takes them straight to `/orders/{reference}`, a **QRIS QR code** generated on the spot at a URL keyed to that payment — reloading the page, backing out and returning, or reopening a bookmark all show the same live QR until it's paid or expires, instead of losing it. Because the guest is paying on the same phone the QR is displayed on, they can't point its camera at its own screen — so the screen also offers **"Download QR"**, saving the code as an image they open from their banking or e-wallet app's "pay from gallery" flow.

Choosing cash sends the guest to the same URL, but showing a **"Bayar di kasir {lokasi}"** instruction screen instead: their daily transaction number as the largest thing on it — the same short number the cashier already reads out and the KDS already prints, easier to say aloud across a counter than the 16-character payment reference underneath it — the amount, the item list, and a countdown to walk downstairs and pay before the order expires. There's nothing to scan and nothing to confirm on the guest's side; paying is the cashier's existing **Pay** flow on the [Transactions](/sales/transactions) list, exactly as it works for a walk-in sale.

Once the bank confirms payment, that same page flips in place to the order-status view — no second navigation, because it was the order-status page all along: the table label (large — it's how the order finds them, there's no pager and no order number), the guest's name, and what they paid for. A `Transaction` now exists for that order, marked paid, showing up on the POS exactly like a cashier-entered sale — badged **"Order App"** and filterable by source — with the right table attached so staff know where to carry it.

That order-status page doesn't stop at "paid" — it keeps watching the order through to pickup. See [Order Fulfilment Status, After Payment](#order-fulfilment-status-after-payment) below. And that page isn't the only way back to this order — every paid order from this session shows up in [Order History](/sales/order-history), one tap away from a header button on every order-app screen.

## Why it matters

Table ordering solved discovery and cart-building, but a guest still had to walk to the counter and pay a cashier — the actual bottleneck at peak hours was untouched. This is what makes self-ordering actually replace a trip to the till, not just make composing the order nicer.

It also had to be built without ever letting a payment credential near the customer's phone. Every DOKU call — minting the QR, checking its status, receiving DOKU's payment notification — happens inside the Go API. The order app talks only to Gatherloop's own endpoints and never holds, or could leak, a gateway secret.

## Order Fulfilment Status, After Payment

Paying doesn't end the guest's page — it starts the part that closes the loop. The same URL that showed the QR now shows a **preparing** screen: the guest's pickup number as the largest thing on it, a pulsing ring and an animated ellipsis proving the page is live, the table, and the item list — with no elapsed timer or countdown anywhere, deliberately. The WhatsApp number collected at checkout means a guest who locks their phone and walks off still gets told the moment their order is ready — see [Guest WhatsApp Notifications](/sales/order-notifications). A barista finishing the order marks it **Ready** from the POS (see [Transactions](/sales/transactions)), and the guest's open page flips itself to a green **ready** screen within about ten seconds, telling them to collect at the counter with their number, whether or not the WhatsApp message has arrived yet. Closing the tab raises no warning of any kind: the guest gets back to this same page from their [order history](/sales/order-history), one tap away on every order-app screen.

## Cancelling a Pending Payment

A guest who picked the wrong method — QRIS when their bank app won't open the saved QR, or cash when they'd rather not walk downstairs — isn't stuck waiting out the timer. Both the QRIS and cash countdown screens carry a secondary **"Batalkan pembayaran"** button below the payment instructions. Tapping it opens a confirmation ("Jika Anda sudah membayar, jangan batalkan…" for QRIS, a cashier-facing note for cash) before anything happens, and pressing the phone's own Back button while the countdown is showing opens the same confirmation instead of silently landing on a cart the guest can't edit. Confirming releases the payment — the cart it was priced from keeps every item, note and quantity — and the guest is back on the cart page, one tap from checking out again with a different method.

Closing the tab instead of pressing Back is covered too: the cart itself now knows when a payment is pending. Reopening the table's QR shows a **"⏳ Menunggu pembayaran…"** bar on the menu in place of the usual cart button, the cart page shows the same items read-only with a banner instead of the checkout button, and opening a menu item to add something explains the pending payment there too, offering **"Batalkan & tambah item"** so cancelling and adding the new item happen in one step. Every one of these surfaces offers **"Lanjutkan pembayaran"** back to the countdown as well, so a guest who just wants to finish paying is never stuck looking at an explanation instead of their QR.

Cancelling is safe against the guest actually having paid: the server re-checks with DOKU before it lets a QRIS cancellation go through, and a cashier can no longer collect cash for an order the guest has walked away from — that pending cash order also drops off the [Kitchen Display System](/sales/kds)'s "waiting at the till" list the moment it's cancelled. If the money turns out to be real after all — a guest cancels, then pays the saved QR PNG anyway — the payment that actually got the money wins, and any newer order made in the meantime is cancelled in its place rather than double-charging.

## Key capabilities

- **QRIS, generated per order** — a fresh, amount-locked dynamic QR from DOKU for exactly what the cart totals at the moment of checkout; a later price edit by staff can never change what the guest is being asked to pay.
- **Save-and-pay-from-gallery** — the guest is paying on the same device showing the QR, so "Download QR" downloads it as a PNG for their banking app's QRIS-from-gallery flow, with a long-press fallback on older iOS Safari.
- **One QR per cart** — a double-tapped pay button or a reloaded screen returns the existing pending QR rather than minting a second one.
- **The QR — or the cash instructions — lives at a URL, not in memory** — `/orders/{reference}` is a durable, reloadable page for that one payment; reloading, backing out and returning, or reopening a bookmark all show the same pending QR or cash instructions (or the paid outcome, once it's paid) instead of stranding the guest back at an empty cart.
- **A second payment method, picked in the same sheet** — "Bayar dengan Cash di Kasir" sits next to "Bayar dengan QRIS" behind `NEXT_PUBLIC_ORDER_CASH_PAYMENT_ENABLED`; with it off, the sheet is byte-for-byte the QRIS-only original.
- **The cashier's existing Pay flow is the cash gateway** — no separate "confirm this app order" screen exists; paying a cash order-app transaction from the [Transactions](/sales/transactions) list settles the payment and flips the guest's screen automatically.
- **A cash order buzzes the kitchen twice** — once at checkout, so a barista can walk to an unstaffed till before the guest arrives, and again once it's paid — see [Kitchen Display System (KDS)](/sales/kds).
- **Cash orders expire on their own too** — a server-side sweeper, not the guest's phone, cancels an unpaid cash order after a configurable walk-and-queue window (`CASH_PAYMENT_EXPIRY_SECONDS`, default 10 minutes — longer than QRIS's QR-validity window, because the clock is measuring a trip to the counter, not a QR's lifetime); the guest's cart is preserved and reordering is one tap.
- **An abandoned order the guest closes the tab on is still findable** — a pending payment, cash or QRIS, shows up in [Order History](/sales/order-history) with a "Belum dibayar" / "Menunggu pembayaran QRIS" badge, routing straight back to the instruction screen or the countdown.
- **Cart freeze while a payment is live, but never a dead end** — while a payment is pending and unexpired, the cart it was priced from can't be mutated; the freeze releases itself the moment the QR or cash window expires, or the guest cancels it themselves (see [Cancelling a Pending Payment](#cancelling-a-pending-payment)) — and every screen the freeze could otherwise silently block explains it and offers a way through.
- **Two independent ways to learn a payment succeeded** — DOKU's own webhook notification is the primary path, and the screen's own status poll independently re-checks with DOKU, so a missed or delayed webhook never leaves a guest staring at a paid QR that the app doesn't know is paid.
- **The server decides expiry, not the phone's clock** — a countdown reaching zero triggers one last check with DOKU before anything is shown as expired, so a few seconds of clock skew can never tell a guest who just paid that their window closed.
- **Remembers the guest's name** — asked once per session, offered again on the next order from the same phone, without ever becoming a login or an account.
- **Origin visible on every transaction** — the POS transaction list and detail screen show a **Source** badge (`POS` / `Order App`) and the table it's headed to, with a matching filter.
- **A wrong payment is fixed the way a wrong cashier payment always was** — no refunds, voids or partial payments exist anywhere in the POS yet, so an order-app mistake is unwound the same manual way (`Unpay` within 24 hours) as one at the till.
- **The status page keeps polling after "paid"** — once the barista marks the order ready on the POS (see [Transactions](/sales/transactions)), the guest's already-open page flips to a pickup screen within about ten seconds, no reload needed.
- **No elapsed time is ever shown to the guest** — the preparing screen proves it's live with motion (a pulsing ring, an animated ellipsis), never a duration or a countdown, so a longer-than-usual wait never reads as the app being stuck.
- **Leaving mid-order raises no confirmation** — closing the tab or navigating away while the order is still preparing is free; the guest finds the order again under their [order history](/sales/order-history) header button, not a dialog telling them to stay.

## For engineers

- Customer-facing screens: `libs/ui/src/presentation/views/screens/order/{CartScreen,OrderStatusScreen}.tsx` — the cart screen owns the pre-payment recap and the "Bayar dengan QRIS" CTA (there is no separate checkout screen), components under `libs/ui/src/presentation/views/components/checkout/`
- Frontend payment state machines, split across the hop: `libs/ui/src/domain/usecases/checkout.ts` owns the name prompt, method selection and payment creation, terminating at `created` with the payment in context; `libs/ui/src/domain/usecases/orderStatus.ts` owns everything about watching one, seeded from its SSR-fetched `payment` — QR polling for QRIS, clock-only polling for cash, the countdown-triggered final check, and the expiry branch. The handoff between them is the navigation to `/orders/{reference}`, not shared in-memory state.
- Route entry: `/t/{code}/cart` (recap and pay, `apps/order-web/src/pages/t/[code]/cart/index.tsx`) and `/orders/{reference}` (`apps/order-web/src/pages/orders/[reference].tsx`) — `/t/{code}/checkout` and the old `/t/{code}/status?ref=` are permanent redirects, not pages
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_ORDER_CHECKOUT_ENABLED` gates the cart's pay button (`libs/ui/src/app/order/Cart.tsx`); when unset the button renders disabled with "Checkout belum tersedia" instead of routing anywhere. `NEXT_PUBLIC_ORDER_CASH_PAYMENT_ENABLED` separately gates the cash option in the name sheet, and `NEXT_PUBLIC_ORDER_CASHIER_LOCATION` (default `Lantai 1`) is rendered in both the sheet and the cash instruction screen.
- Gateway integration, entirely server-side: `apps/api/data/doku/**` implements `PaymentGatewayRepository` (`apps/api/domain/payment_repository.go`) against DOKU's SNAP QRIS Direct API — the only layer that ever holds a DOKU credential. Cash never touches this port: `Payment.RequiresGateway()` (`apps/api/domain/payment_entity.go`) is the one branch point, so every "should I call DOKU?" question fails closed for a future third method.
- Checkout + confirmation usecases: `apps/api/domain/payment_usecase.go` (`Checkout`, `ConfirmPayment`, `GetPaymentStatus`, `ExpireStalePayments` — the server-side sweeper that expires an abandoned cash or QRIS payment even if nobody's phone is watching)
- Cash settles through the cashier's own pay flow: `TransactionUsecase.PayTransaction` (`apps/api/domain/transaction_usecase.go`) calls `settleOrderPayment`, which flips the linked `payments` row to paid and converts the cart — the same `PUT /transactions/{id}/pay` route a walk-in sale already uses
- Backend routes: `POST /carts/current/checkout` (`method: qris \| cash`), `GET /payments/{partnerReferenceNo}`, `GET /payments` (order history, including every pending payment, any method), `POST /payments/{partnerReferenceNo}/cancel` (owner session only, idempotent), and the unauthenticated-but-signature-verified `POST /payments/doku/notification` (`apps/api/presentation/restapi/payment_route.go`, `VerifyDokuSignature` middleware)
- Cancel: `PaymentUsecase.CancelPayment` (`apps/api/domain/payment_usecase.go`) re-queries DOKU before giving up on a QRIS payment, then routes through the same `finalizeUncollectedPayment` that expiry uses, so there is one definition of "releasing a payment"; a `pending` payment paid late, or a cashier's late cash collection, supersedes any newer pending payment on the same cart rather than allowing a double order. `Cart.pendingPayment` (`apps/api/domain/cart_entity.go`, `CartUsecase.GetCurrentCart`) carries the lock onto every cart response, which is what lets the menu, item sheet, cart and cart-item-edit screens explain it without a second request. `ORDER_PAYMENT_CANCEL_ENABLED` is the kill switch (`apps/api/utils/env.go`).
- Frontend: `libs/ui/src/domain/usecases/paymentCancel.ts` (`PaymentCancelUsecase`, `idle → confirming → cancelling → settled`) is shared by `OrderStatusHandler`, `MenuListHandler` and `CartHandler` through `presentation/handlers/hooks/usePaymentCancel.ts`; `presentation/views/components/checkout/PaymentCancelAlert.tsx` is the confirmation dialog, and `presentation/views/components/cart/{PendingPaymentBar,PendingPaymentNotice}.tsx` are the locked-cart surfaces on the menu and everywhere else. `utils/backNavigationGuard.ts` (`installBackNavigationGuard`, web-only, wrapping Next's `Router.beforePopState`) turns a Back press into the same confirmation.
- Design doc: `docs/prd-order-payment-cancellation.md` — why a cancel is a new terminal `payments.status` rather than reusing `expired` (D1), why the cart is never touched by a cancel (D2), the race against a paid-late webhook and a cashier's own pay flow (D6-D8), and why the cart carries its own lock instead of a second endpoint (D18)
- Fulfilment status is `transactions.completed_at`, a nullable timestamp read through the `Payment.fulfillmentStatus` field `ToApiPayment` derives (`apps/api/presentation/restapi/payment_transformer.go`) — the guest never reads `transactions` directly
- `OrderStatusUsecase` (`libs/ui/src/domain/usecases/orderStatus.ts`) splits `loaded` into `preparing` (polls every 10s) and `ready` (terminal, stops polling); the two screens are `OrderPreparingView` / `OrderReadyView` (`libs/ui/src/presentation/views/components/orderStatus/`)
- Design doc: `docs/prd-order-fulfillment-status.md` — the barista/guest state model (`completed_at` on `transactions`, D1/D2), why no duration is ever rendered (D17), and what a future kitchen display system inherits from this design
- Transaction origin: `source` (`pos` \| `order`) and `cart_id` on `transactions` (migration `000023`), surfaced in `libs/ui/src/presentation/views/components/transactions/{TransactionListItem,TransactionDetail}.tsx`
- Guest identity: `customers` table keyed by session id (migration `000024`) — a display name and, since `docs/prd-order-whatsapp-notifications.md`, a WhatsApp number; still no email or cross-session identity
- Design doc: `docs/prd-order-checkout-qris-doku.md` — every decision behind the above, including why the transaction is created unpaid at QR generation rather than on payment success (D4), how an abandoned checkout is disposed of (D5), and what's still explicitly out of scope (refunds, other payment methods, kitchen notifications)
- Follow-up design doc: `docs/prd-order-app-ux-round-2.md` — moved checkout from its own page onto the cart page and gave the QR a durable, reloadable URL on the status page (D4, D5), retiring the old `/checkout` route
- Follow-up design doc: `docs/prd-order-whatsapp-notifications.md` — the WhatsApp number collected at checkout and the order-ready message it triggers, replacing an earlier push-notification opt-in; see [Guest WhatsApp Notifications](/sales/order-notifications)
- Follow-up design doc: `docs/prd-order-cash-payment.md` — why cash is a `payments.method` value rather than a new table (D1), why the cashier's existing pay flow is the cash gateway (D11), the separate cash expiry window and why it's twice QRIS's (D5), and why a cash-pending KDS notification bypasses the station rule (D9)
